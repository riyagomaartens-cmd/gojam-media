FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a video cost?

Our packages start from R2,499 for a social media reel, R4,499 for a brand video, R4,500 for a music video and R8,000 for a wedding film. Every project can be tailored, and we're happy to put together a custom quote for anything that doesn't fit a package.

How much does a monthly content retainer cost?

Retainers start from R9,899 a month and run for a minimum of 3 months. Consistency is what builds an audience, and three months gives the content time to work. Bundles can be adjusted to what your team needs.

How do I book?

The easiest way is to book a free strategy call. We'll talk through your goals, recommend the right package and send you a quote. If you'd rather not call, you can send us an enquiry instead.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit secures your shoot date, and the balance is due before final delivery.

I'm not an artist, I'm a business that needs professional marketing. Is Go Jam right for me?

Yes. Creativity is for everyone, and it's one of the best tools a business has. Content that's bold and out of the box is what sets you apart from competitors who all look the same. We bring the same creative energy to brands and businesses as we do to music.

Do you help with ideas and scripts?

Yes. Before a camera rolls, we work through your goals and audience, then put together concepts, scripts and a shoot plan for you to sign off on. You don't need to arrive with everything figured out.

Do you offer photography?

Yes. Our photography is handled by a trusted network of professional photographers we work with, so you get the right specialist for your shoot while we manage the whole process.

How long until I get my content?

Turnaround starts once filming is complete. Most reels are delivered within 5-7 days, music videos and brand videos within 7-14 days, and wedding films within 2-4 weeks. Need it faster? Rush delivery in 24-48 hours is available as an add-on.

How many revisions do I get?

Every package includes at least one revision, and higher tiers include two or three. Extra revisions are available if you need them.

Can I get the raw footage?

Yes. Raw footage delivery is available as an add-on for R2,499.

Can you film in Johannesburg or internationally?

Yes, travel is always an option. Shoots more than 50km from Cape Town are charged at AA travel rates, and for larger local or international productions, please get in touch privately so we can plan it properly.

Not sure which package fits?

Tell us your goal and budget and we'll recommend the right tier, or build you a custom quote. First conversation is always free.
