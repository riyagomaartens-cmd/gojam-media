Why we exist

Great work deserves to be seen.

Van Gogh sold one painting in his lifetime. His work was good enough — the problem was that nobody saw it.

Today, being seen means being consistent, with visuals that match the quality of what you make. We don't change what you create. We change how the world sees it.

Our work spans music videos and release content, brand videos and social reels, corporate interviews and podcasts, events and weddings, photography, and monthly content retainers. Every project combines bold visuals with strategic thinking — to stop the scroll and turn viewers into customers.

Meet the founder

Behind the lens.

Go Jam Media was founded in 2023 by Riyago Maartens, a Cape Town videographer, editor and Director of Photography. He won Best Director and Best Editor at the international FilmIt competition, and today he also directs visual production at Ipic Media for content that reaches over a million views every month.

Go Jam runs as a lean, founder-led studio — bringing in trusted extra hands when a project calls for it, so every shoot keeps the same standard:
