Privacy

How this website handles visits, enquiries and bookings. Last updated 13 October 2026.

Go Jam Media keeps this simple. We only use the tools below, and only to run the site and respond to you.

Counting visits

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count how many people visit each page. It does not use cookies and does not collect personal data or build a profile of you. It just helps us see which pages are useful.

Enquiries and bookings

If you fill in the enquiry form it is handled by Jotform, and if you book a call it is handled by Cal.com. The details you enter (such as your name, email and what you tell us about your project) are used only to reply to you and arrange the work. They are not sold or used for advertising.

Questions

For any privacy question, email riyago@ipicmedia.co.za and we'll help.
